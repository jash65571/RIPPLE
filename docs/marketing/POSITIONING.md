# Positioning brief

## Audience

Adults who enjoy short logic puzzles, readable systems, and experimenting without a timer.

## Value

Watch a small harbor delay, change one allowed route or start time, and test whether the whole morning improves.

## Differentiator

Later puzzles test the same plan against two or three known versions of the day. The full event trace is visible, so every wait has an inspectable cause.

## Verified scope

Ten deterministic levels, a complete ending, local saves, import and export, offline play after the first load, keyboard and touch controls, adjustable text and motion, optional sound, no account, no ads, and no gameplay telemetry.
