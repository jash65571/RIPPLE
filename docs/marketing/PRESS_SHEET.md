# Press sheet

## Summary

RIPPLE is a working title for a compact browser puzzle about clearing a chain of harbor delays. Players inspect an event trace, change one or two starting rules, and test the result across up to three possible days.

## Features

- Ten original deterministic puzzles and a complete ending
- One visible plan tested across several futures
- Undo, redo, rewind, comparison, hints, and recovery tools
- Local saves with import and export
- Keyboard, touch, structured text play, text scaling, and reduced motion
- Installable offline PWA with no account, ads, or gameplay telemetry

## Controls

Buttons choose routes and start beats. Space pauses or resumes. Z undoes. Y redoes. Timeline controls step, rewind, change speed, and scrub.

## Platforms and status

The review build runs in current desktop Chrome and responsive browser layouts. Firefox, WebKit, physical phone, assistive-technology, host, and store acceptance remain unverified. The final product name, publisher identity, public URL, support route, and policies require owner approval.
