# Four-week zero-spend launch plan

## Stop conditions

Do not launch while the name, owner identity, support route, public URL, or policy review is unresolved. Stop if physical-device acceptance finds a blocking input or layout defect. Stop if the hosted build fails its header, offline, or update checks.

## Week 1

Owner action: publish the approved site and one factual introduction on owned channels. Success signal: players can open the game and report a specific first-level experience.

## Week 2

Owner action: invite voluntary feedback in communities that permit self-promotion. Success signal: useful reports about controls, difficulty, and favorite moments.

## Week 3

Owner action: ship a verified improvement with a short change note. Success signal: fewer repeated usability complaints and some voluntary return play.

## Week 4

Owner action: decide whether a second chapter deserves work. Success signal: direct interest in more puzzles, not view counts alone.

No advertising spend, bulk messages, bought votes, or manufactured reviews are part of this plan.
