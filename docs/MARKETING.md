# Marketing kit

## Positioning

RIPPLE is a calm cause-and-effect puzzle game about finding the one small schedule change that clears a busy harbor. It is for players who enjoy readable systems, compact levels, and testing a plan against several possible futures.

## Store copy

Short: Watch a harbor delay, change one rule, and test whether the whole morning still works.

Medium: A bus, parcel robot, and market cart share a small harbor. Rewind each delay, change a route or departure time, then test the same plan across every possible day. Ten hand-built puzzles, local saves, offline play, and no account or ads.

Long: Mornings in this harbor run on tiny decisions. A bus enters a crossing first. A robot waits. A market cart blocks the next lane. Watch the event trace, inspect each vehicle, and change only what the puzzle allows. Later levels introduce closures, linked departures, and several possible versions of the day. Your plan has to survive them all. RIPPLE includes ten deterministic puzzles, undo and recovery tools, a complete text view, keyboard and touch controls, adjustable motion and text, local progress export, and offline installation.

## Tags and categories

Puzzle, logic, strategy, casual, single player, family friendly, cozy, minimalist, time management, systems puzzle, browser game, offline.

## Press note

RIPPLE turns traffic timing into a compact logic toy. Players watch a small harbor schedule unfold, change one route or start time, and test the plan against up to three possible days. The first release has ten original levels and runs free in a browser without accounts, ads, or gameplay telemetry.
