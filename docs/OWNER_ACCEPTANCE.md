# Five-minute owner acceptance

1. Open `/play/` in a private browser window and start Level 01.
2. Watch, pause, rewind, scrub, change to Garden path, test, and continue.
3. Open Help, close it with Escape, switch to text view, and select a vehicle detail.
4. Open Settings, change text size and motion, export progress, and cancel a clear.
5. Open Level 10 from Chapters, inspect all three possible days, reveal the solution, restore the prior plan, then solve it.
6. Reload and confirm the plan and completion remain.
7. Wait for Offline ready, disconnect the network, reload Level 10, and play it.
8. Restore the network and open every footer page.

Also test install and sound on one physical iPhone or iPad and one physical Android phone. Confirm touch targets, safe-area spacing, orientation changes, browser back behavior, audio after interruption, and home-screen launch. Those checks cannot be proven by desktop emulation.
