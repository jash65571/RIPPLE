A puzzle about what happens next

RIPPLE is a ten-level, single-player browser game. Each puzzle begins with a harbor plan that misses at least one deadline. You can inspect the events, rewind them, and change a small number of routes or departure times.

Why a harbor?

A bus, parcel robot, and market cart make cause and effect easy to see. Shared crossings create queues. A raised bridge creates a wait. Later puzzles ask one plan to work across two or three possible days.

What is included

The first chapter has exactly ten hand-built puzzles, local saves, three-stage hints, a text play view, and offline installation. It has no accounts, ads, payments, tracking pixels, or online leaderboard.
