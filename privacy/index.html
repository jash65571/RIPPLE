Privacy draft

Private review draft, effective September 15, 2026. The operator name and public contact route are not configured.

Game progress on your device

RIPPLE stores completed levels, unfinished plans, settings, and limited undo history in IndexedDB in your browser. The game does not send this progress to an account or analytics service. You can export a JSON copy or clear progress from Settings.

Hosting requests

A future static hosting provider may process IP addresses, request times, browser details, and security metadata to deliver and protect the site. The final provider settings and retention information must be verified before publication.

Support

No support form sends data in this review build. If a support contact is added, messages will contain whatever the sender chooses to include. Bug reports should omit names, tokens, and private files.

Your choices

You can play without an account. You can remove local game data through Settings or browser controls. Exported files remain under your control. A public contact route for privacy questions and applicable rights must be added before release.
