Watch, change, test

1. Watch the original plan

Use Play, Pause, Previous event, and Next event to inspect the delay. Time is measured in beats. A resource freed at beat 3 can be entered at beat 3.

2. Change a starting rule

Select a named route or departure beat. The change counter tracks fields that differ from the original plan. Returning a field to its original value refunds the change.

3. Test every possible day

Later puzzles have two or three futures. Your single shared plan must meet every displayed deadline. Changing the plan marks old results as Test again.

Queues and ties

Shared segments hold one vehicle at a time. Earlier arrivals stay first. If arrivals tie, the bus goes first, then the robot, then the cart. Vehicles wait safely in bays and never collide.

Saves

Progress is stored in this browser. Export it from Settings if you want a backup. Browser storage can be cleared, and progress is not synced to another device.
