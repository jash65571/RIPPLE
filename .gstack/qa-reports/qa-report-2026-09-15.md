## Findings fixed

- P1: PixiJS application destruction unloaded shared text textures during plan changes and blanked the React root. Fixed by retaining one renderer through scene updates and avoiding child texture destruction.
- P2: Phone board toolbar clipped the final zoom control. Fixed with wrapping controls and mobile flex sizing.
- P2: Marketing captures included the one-time offline-ready notice. Fixed by dismissing transient notices in the capture flow.
- P2: Help and solution overlays lacked native focus containment and Escape handling. Replaced with native modal dialogs.
- P2: Unknown level-version links silently opened current data. They now show the safe puzzle-not-found route.
- P2: Settings checkboxes could snap back while their save completed. They now update immediately and persist in the background.

## Remaining concerns

- Firefox and WebKit automation are blocked by browser-download timeouts.
- Physical mobile and assistive-technology acceptance needs owner hardware.
- Final name, legal identity, support contact, and production host are not configured.

## Result

DONE_WITH_CONCERNS. All 12 Chromium stories pass. No known P0 or P1 application defect remains in the tested review build.
